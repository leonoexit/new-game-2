How to play
  • Select Character + Fishing Rod, then tap a Fish. A valid Catch always succeeds. Silver Minnow costs 1 AP; Blue Eel costs 2 AP.
  • Leaving means this fish will leave after you catch one other fish. Catch it now to keep it.
  • Lingering fish can stay through more Catches. The number below each card is how many other Catches remain before it leaves.
  • Each successful Catch advances every other fish by one step, even when it costs 2 AP. Chosen fish are caught before departures resolve.
  • New fish fill empty slots after departures. Arrivals do not lose a step immediately. The test has 6 fish in total; arrivals stop when the queue is empty.
  • Reading, choosing sources, invalid actions and returning to Farm do not advance fish. The river stays saved while you visit Farm in this test.
  • There is no real-time timer, random failure or free refresh. Rain is fixed so Blue Eel can appear.
