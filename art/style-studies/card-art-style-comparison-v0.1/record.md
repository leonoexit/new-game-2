# Card art style comparison v0.1

- Purpose: compare four visual languages on the same three V0 subjects before choosing any new production direction.
- Status: `study` for all four boards. None is an approved reference or runtime asset.
- Date: 2026-09-26.
- Tool: built-in `image_gen`; model version and seed unavailable.
- Shared subjects, left to right: Hedgerow with blue berries; Mature Turnip Crop with three white bulbs and violet shoulders; cream Farmhouse with terracotta roof and blue shutters.
- Generated board format: 2172 × 724 pixels, three square panels per board. These boards are comparison sheets, not card artwork delivered for runtime.
- Reference roles: the project's [approved farm pixel image](../../approved/style-references/farm-high-key-style-reference-v0.1.png) informed the refined-pixel hypothesis by visual inspection; the owner's toy-card image attached in the 2026-09-26 conversation inspired only the bold-outline hypothesis. Neither image was passed directly to the generator or copied into a generated board.
- Source boards: [refined pixel](source/refined-pixel-three-subjects-v0.1.png), [storybook gouache](source/storybook-gouache-three-subjects-v0.1.png), [cut paper](source/cut-paper-three-subjects-v0.1.png), [bold-outline kawaii](source/bold-outline-kawaii-three-subjects-v0.1.png).
- Processing: each full board was resized to 480 × 160 pixels for a 160-pixel-wide panel check. Refined pixel used ImageMagick point filtering; the other styles used Lanczos. No crop, repainting, or compositing was applied.
- Preview boards: [refined pixel](processed/refined-pixel-preview-480x160-v0.1.png), [storybook gouache](processed/storybook-gouache-preview-480x160-v0.1.png), [cut paper](processed/cut-paper-preview-480x160-v0.1.png), [bold-outline kawaii](processed/bold-outline-kawaii-preview-480x160-v0.1.png).

## Review at 160 pixels per subject

- **Refined pixel:** strongest continuity with the approved game world. Hedgerow berries, turnip bulbs, and the farmhouse door remain distinct. The scenery still occupies considerable visual space.
- **Storybook gouache:** warmest atmosphere and strongest sense of a lived-in place. Soft edges and busy foliage weaken the hedgerow/turnip silhouette at small size.
- **Cut paper:** clear layered shapes and a calmer surface, although this first board retains more background detail than the hypothesis called for.
- **Bold-outline kawaii:** strongest immediate subject separation and mobile readability. Its playful toy-like energy may shift the world's tone away from the existing farm setting; evaluate that preference with the owner, not as a technical defect.
- All four preserve the same basic subject identities, but generated compositions differ slightly. The comparison tests direction, not final production consistency or state-to-state continuity.
- Approval: pending owner review. Do not assign these studies to the Card Register or playable build.
