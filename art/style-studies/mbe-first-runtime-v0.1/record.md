## Generation prompts

The built-in tool received each subject-specific sentence below inside this shared instruction: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production-candidate square raster illustration for the approved MBE-like visual direction of Little Valley Cards. The attached three-subject board is our own continuity reference; the owner's MBE sample is a broad style reference only. [SUBJECT] Compose an original image with the subject occupying roughly 72% of the square and comfortable margins so the full square can be displayed without any crop, masking, or background removal. Use a lively but restrained green, cobalt, violet, cream, coral palette. Avoid copied reference characters, recognizable brand motifs, educational-poster look, infographic layout, gradients and generic emoji styling. Output image only.”

- **Hedgerow [SUBJECT]:** “Create ONE original square 1:1 full-bleed Little Valley Cards artwork for the HEDGEROW in its Berries ready state. A low rooted leafy hedge fills the center with exactly three highly visible cobalt berry clusters. It must read as a berry bush, not loose fruit or a branch being held. Use a warm off-white entire square canvas, not transparency. Rounded geometric masses, a few bold colored outlines with deliberate breaks, playful compact baseline strokes and very sparse small accents. Charming and warm, clear at 160px. No face, no person, no text, no frame, no labels, no detached stickers, no scene, no shadows.”
- **Turnip Crop [SUBJECT]:** “Create ONE original square 1:1 full-bleed Little Valley Cards artwork for a MATURE TURNIP CROP. Three stout white round turnips with violet shoulders and chunky green tops stand partly in one small shared soil patch. Middle turnip may have a tiny friendly face; no other face. Keep the roots visibly mature and harvestable. Use a warm off-white entire square canvas, not transparency. Rounded geometric masses, a few bold colored outlines with deliberate breaks, playful compact baseline strokes and very sparse small accents. Clear at 160px. No text, no frame, no labels, no detached stickers, no landscape, no shadows.”
- **Farmhouse [SUBJECT]:** “Create ONE original square 1:1 full-bleed Little Valley Cards artwork for the FARMHOUSE place. A compact squat cream cottage with a terracotta roof, one blue-shuttered window, and one clearly visible blue front door. It is a house, not a character: no face. Use a warm off-white entire square canvas, not transparency. Rounded geometric masses, a few bold colored outlines with deliberate breaks, tiny warm accent dots and compact baseline marks. Clear at 160px. No text, no frame, no labels, no detached stickers, no landscape or neighboring buildings, no shadows.”
