## 2. Bram: conserved K against Q4

The owner-decided earlier K boundary conserves one Work action's total Progress and does not improve Progress/AP. Q4 Water Trough and Harvest Bench each require 4 Progress; one ordinary Work is 1 AP for 2 Progress. Two normal Work finish either Project and four finish both. Splitting `1+1` changes which partial bars are lit but cannot finish both with fewer than four actions (`8 required / 2 per action = 4`). Because each target is even, `1+1` can delay the first completed relief compared with assigning two early Work to it. Focusing `2` on one is identical to ordinary Work. PJ-X has no playable reward or quantity and cannot supply a purpose by assumption.

Bram remains a Person with an **open activated ability**. The next meaningful choice is whether to preserve K's no-output-increase role and accept that V0 lacks a useful target, or authorize a different role/boundary after a stronger native case is found. This paper test does not make that product decision.
