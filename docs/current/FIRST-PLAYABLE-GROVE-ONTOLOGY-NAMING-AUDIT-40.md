## 5. Owner's source-game reference

`[INFORMED-OWNER REFERENCE]` In the original game, Wild Herb is generally encountered visibly during free roaming in places associated with trees/woods, rather than in town, at sea, inside the farmhouse, or in the mine. This explains the **experience** the owner is pointing to: noticing a forage item in a suitable environment and choosing to pick it up. It does not establish a named `Grove` location or a `Grove Batch` reveal action.

`[OWNER CLARIFICATION FOR THIS GAME]` Little Valley Cards will **not** have actual free roaming. This is consistent with [Area model 08](SCROLLABLE-AREA-TABLEAU-MODEL-08.md): scrolling/focusing the card tableau is UI navigation, not character travel. The original-game reference can inform *where an item feels appropriate* and *that the opportunity is noticed visually* without importing movement mechanics.

The simplest unresolved question for this project is now: **what does the player see on the card table when a Wild Herb is available, and what do they act on to pick it up?** A visible Herb opportunity as an on-table target in a tree-associated context is a plausible **paper presentation candidate**, not yet an owner-selected card or host Area. Do not answer by silently inventing a forest Area, a movement verb or a Search action. The `G-V` AP test can remain on hold while that visible surface is decided.

## 7. Focused board-game reference and foraging proposal

`[BOARD-GAME RULE, NOT THIS GAME'S RULE]` The [official *Stardew Valley: The Board Game* rulebook, p. 11](https://www.dropbox.com/s/ek7h6zn9ul2gxp1/Stardew%20Rules%20v2%20for%20Download.pdf?dl=0), linked from the [publisher's store](https://shop.stardewvalley.net/), puts facedown Forageable/Tree tiles by paths. During an action–move–action turn, moving along a bordering path may claim one such tile; the pickup does not use either of the turn's two actions. The [board-game designer explains](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/a-huge-list-of-questions-desired-clarifications.6619/page-2) that movement represents a day's focus on one or two locations; cleared paths may remain empty until later replenishment. These facts describe the board game, not the original video game's visible ground items.

`[TRANSFERABLE IDEA]` A forage item is a **bounded opportunity associated with an outdoor context**, and taking it competes with what the player chooses to do that day. The board game's exact trigger is movement. Little Valley Cards has no movement; scrolling is free UI navigation. A free pickup triggered by scrolling would therefore have none of the board game's route/action constraint. The board game also hides tile identity, whereas V0 currently authors only Wild Herb as an optional forage output, so a hidden batch would add little informed choice.

`[CORRECTION AFTER OWNER CHALLENGE]` The assistant first described an Herb card appearing without specifying when, then invented a forage source/draw step to fill the gap. That step is unnecessary for the collection interaction. The `G-V` AP/cadence calculations remain conditional examples; they do not establish item appearance timing.

`[OWNER-PROPOSED INTERACTION]` An eligible forage item, such as **Wild Herb**, appears as its own visible card directly within a suitable Area. It is a world item while on the tableau, not yet an inventory item. Stack the reusable base Tool **Hand / Bàn tay** onto that exact item and pay the shown AP to collect it; the world card is removed and one corresponding inventory item is gained. The same Hand stacked onto a **Mature Crop** performs Harvest. No separate Forage card, hidden result draw, intermediate source card, `Grove` Area, or path movement is required for this interaction. The Area determines which forage items may appear; the visible item card is the target.

`[AP BOUNDARY]` The owner explicitly keeps an AP cost for collecting a forage item. One Hand use targets one eligible card in the current paper fixture. The previous 1-AP Collect and 1-AP Harvest calculations are candidate amounts, not newly selected values. Stacking Hand must not silently change Harvest capacity or make every card in an Area collectible.

`[EARLIER OPEN QUESTION — ANSWERED FOR WILD HERB IN §8]` At this stage, Area, timing, quantity and expiry were still missing. Section 8 now defines them for V0 Wild Herb specifically; it does not define the appearance rules for every future forage item. Wild Herb need not already exist in inventory to appear in the world.

## 8. Original-game reference and V0 appearance decision (2026-09-24)

`[ORIGINAL VIDEO GAME]` The [official Stardew Valley Wiki's Foraging page](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Foraging) describes visible ground forage that can appear overnight on eligible outdoor tiles, with a per-map weekly limit and cleanup before Sunday and a new Season. Its [Cindersap Forest](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Cindersap_Forest) and [Backwoods](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Backwoods) pages show wooded locations with different forage by Season. These pages are on the developer-hosted official wiki, which [ConcernedApe says is community-edited](https://www.stardewvalley.net/stardew-valley-wiki-ownership-change/). The original game also has forage at other outdoor locations, including town and beach; restricting **our** Wild Herb to a wooded Area is a project-specific choice aligned with the owner's source-game experience, not a claim that all original-game forage is forest-only. `Wild Herb` is our paper item name, not the name of a particular original-game plant.

`[V0 PAPER BASELINE — DECISION REQUESTED BY OWNER]` Add one abstract **Rìa Rừng / Woodland Edge Area** to the vertical strip. This is a presentation/rulescope with trees, not a map destination or travel action. Place the existing Hedgerow and Woodlot cards there. **Wild Herb may appear only in this Area** in V0, directly as one visible world-item card; not in Home/Farm, Commerce, River or Mine. It is available in every Season of the V0 paper model, standing in for the source game's changing seasonal forest forage until more distinct plants are authored. `Grove Batch` is no longer a required player-facing source card; its earlier approved name remains historical paper identity.

`[V0 PAPER GENERATOR]` At the start of each Season, shuffle five appearance slips: one `Wild Herb`, four `no new Herb`. On each New Day, **if no uncollected Wild Herb card is already in Rìa Rừng**, reveal and consume the next slip. A Herb slip places one face-up Wild Herb world card; a blank slip changes nothing. Reshuffle all five slips only after the fifth has been consumed. When an Herb card remains on the tableau, pause draws rather than consuming slips. This is automatic day setup, never an AP Search or a player draw. The deck is a compact paper translation of overnight appearance and bounded supply, **not** a claim that the video game uses a one-in-five rate. The first appearance occurs by D5 if the Area is empty on D1; with prompt collection, a full 18-day Season offers three or four appearance opportunities. A collected Herb enters inventory and may persist there under normal storage rules.

`[PERSISTENCE AND COLLECTION]` At most one uncollected Wild Herb card may occupy this Area. It stays visible through Sleep until the player places Hand on it and pays **1 AP** to Collect, or until the Season changes. At the Season boundary, remove any uncollected card, reset and reshuffle the five slips, and make the incoming Season's first New-Day check. Seeing or leaving the card does not create inventory. This retains the owner's AP requirement and reuses the previous 1-AP paper amount, while adopting the original game's multi-day persistence and Season cleanup in a smaller card scale. It replaces the earlier automatic Sleep expiry and abstract `G-V` source-card wording.

`[CONSEQUENCE]` Under Season-long persistence, Nell's earlier one-Sleep **Hold Open** ability has no normal Wild Herb target; old D13→D14 Hold examples in tests 36–38 are historical comparators, not valid cases for this baseline. [Package v1 §7.1–7.2](YEAR-1-PAPER-DESIGN-PACKAGE-V1.md) records the withdrawn crop-only Harvest Assist and Nell's current general 2-AP relief rule. Herb still persists through Sleep; Hand Collect's normal cost is 1 AP, subject to Nell's general relief when active. The earlier five-day clock test is not proof of this shuffled generator's balance. No new art, implementation or fresh-player claim follows from this paper decision.

## 9. Focused 18-day sanity check of the new rule

This checks only the new appearance/persistence contract, not the full farm or Person balance. With one Herb slip among five and a daily New-Day check while the slot is empty, the first world card must appear on D1–D5. It survives a busy day and can be collected later; no AP is charged for blank appearance slips. After Hand + 1 AP Collect, the next appearance check is the following New Day, not immediately after pickup. An uncollected card blocks further slips and is cleared at the Season boundary before the incoming Season's fresh deck/check.

Under the **historical** all-Sunny farm route and Nell D4/D10/D16 appearance fixture from [test 38](../evidence/FIRST-PLAYABLE-WILD-HERB-CADENCE-PAPER-TEST-38.md), earliest available Collect/Gift pairs for the five possible first-slip positions are:

| First Herb appears | Earliest Collect while preserving that fixture route | First Nell Gift in that fixture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1–D4 | D4 | D4, if the player spends both Collect and Gift AP and shifts D4 route work as in test 38 |
| D5 | D6 | D10 |

The card's persistence is why a D3 or D5 appearance is now recoverable; the old one-day opportunity test did not have that property. For a separate **prompt same-day collection** ceiling, four independent five-slip orderings across 18 days produce three Herb appearances when the fourth Herb slip lands after D18, or four when it lands by D18. There are 250 and 375 such orderings respectively out of 625 equally likely ordered-block combinations. Delayed collection pauses draws, so this is not a guaranteed three-Herb supply. The only guaranteed appearance for an empty first Season is the first one by D5. This check does not select Nell's schedule or Gift cost/Heart values. Her current ability is specified separately in [package v1 §7.2](YEAR-1-PAPER-DESIGN-PACKAGE-V1.md).
