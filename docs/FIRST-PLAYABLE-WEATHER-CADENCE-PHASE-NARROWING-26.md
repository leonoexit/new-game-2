- `[OWNER CLARIFICATION — TV SOURCE]` TV is a selectable sub-card revealed inside Farmhouse. Playing TV on day D costs 0 AP and reports Weather for D+1 on TV. Sleep uses that forecast without a second Weather draw. If the player did not play TV, Sleep determines D+1 Weather itself; it does **not** open or play TV. That Sleep result cannot inform earlier D allocations.
- `[OWNER SELECTED WEATHER DIRECTION]` Rain is generated by RNG conditioned on the **Season of the day being generated**. Rain dates and the number of Rain days in a Season are not fixed. At a Season boundary, the D+1 outcome uses the incoming Season's Weather parameters. TV and Sleep invoke the same one-outcome rule above; the exact seasonal probabilities, any dependence between days, and starting-day setup are still open. This direction does not select a visible Weather deck or a dry-gap guarantee.

Renaming Shortroot/Tallbean/Dewleaf and River Minnow/Rain Eel to the canonical paper names did not change these trace conditions.
