## 1. The actual V0 content boundary

`Grove Batch` is an approved **source-card name**, and `Wild Herb` is the only currently specified Grove-specific output in V0. Hedgerow Berry and Wood already have named deterministic sources. The older resource-area simulation's six-card Wood/Berry/Fiber/Mushroom batches, `Search Grove 1 AP`, and daily Search cap were explicitly **fixtures**, not approved V0 contents or costs. PJ-X's Upper Grove has no defined playable reward and cannot be used as a second batch result.

The current question is therefore narrower than “how many different Grove rewards can a future game support?”: with one authored optional item, should a player pay to reveal whether that item exists today, or see the opportunity and decide whether to Collect it?

## 3. `G-V` paper grammar and recovery

1. At daily setup, the **abstract Wild Herb source opportunity** is visible if present. Otherwise there is no collectible target. This does not decide whether the opportunity is a separate card or a state on the source card, or whether Grove is an Area. A blank day offers no paid action to discover the same blank result.
2. Inspecting the shown opportunity, its item/state, `Collect 1 AP` paper cost and Sleep expiry is free. `Collect` consumes 1 AP and creates one `Fresh · Forage · Herb` item in storage. It is never created merely by seeing or holding the opportunity.
3. An uncollected Wild Herb opportunity normally expires at Sleep. A previously acquired Nell ability may hold **one shown, uncollected card** through one Sleep; it still costs 1 AP to Collect next day, cannot be held repeatedly, and cannot cross a Season boundary under the current C fixture.
4. Once Collected, `S∞` storage applies: the item can be Kept, Gifted, Sold through end-of-day Shipping Bin payout, or recorded in Field Notes when eligible. Recording does not consume it. No Project or base verb requires this Herb as its sole mandatory source.
5. Wild Herb may appear again in a later eligible Grove cycle. The generation rule, seasonal eligibility, interval/probability and first available day are **open**; this paper contract promises a recovery route, not a maximum wait or a specific D8/D13 spawn. Do not infer an 18-day quota from the Person appearance fixture.

This grammar is a conditional **interaction proposal**, not a claim that a daily `G-V` generator or a Grove Area already exists. A normal V0 player can access the Wild Herb source, Farm, River, Mine and Field Notes without owning Mira or Nell.
