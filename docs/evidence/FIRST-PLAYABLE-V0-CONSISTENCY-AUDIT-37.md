## 3. Arithmetic and dependency checks

- Q4 M/B: two ordinary 1-AP Work actions × 2 Progress = 4 Progress per Project. Four Work across M+B produce exactly 8 of 8 required Progress. Splitting one action as 1+1 changes allocation only; with 4/4 requirements it does not save a Work. A bonus Progress or free Work would change the earlier owner boundary.
- Turnip: first Harvest D3; replant and Care on D3, second Care D4, next Harvest D5, then D7. A D3 purchase of two 2G Seeds needs 4G **before** Shipping Bin payout. The 4G/2G numbers belong to a price fixture.
- Nell's held Grove card remains an opportunity, not inventory. Under `G-V`, a D13 full farm day can lead to a D14 1-AP Collect only if Nell was already acquired and had Heart; a D13 appearance alone cannot fund her first Gift. D8/D13 Herb is scenario input, not generator evidence.
- Ordinary Wild Herb has no edge through PJ-X. Upper Grove's optional context cannot be counted as a source, reward, Mira target, or Bram target until specified. Blue Eel remains optional under possible no-Rain S18 outcomes.
