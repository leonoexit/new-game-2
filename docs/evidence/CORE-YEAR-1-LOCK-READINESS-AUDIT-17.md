## 3. Dependency between the two remaining paper-lock workstreams

Canonical identity and numerical balance can both continue at paper level, but neither should silently promote the other's fixtures:

- identity work may choose names, ownership and final content count without turning current prices/durations into laws;
- balance work may compare exact numbers using the shared fixture vocabulary without making Shortroot, Tallbean, Dewleaf, Mira, Nell, Bram or the current Project names canonical;
- final product lock eventually needs both streams reconciled;
- fresh-player evidence remains a separate evidence gate and cannot be manufactured by more informed-owner review.

Therefore G0 does not itself select which paper-lock stream should run next.

## 4. Sequencing arms

### `L-I — identity/quantity first`

Next narrow the canonical minimum content set: crop/Catch/source/Project/Person identities, final role assignment and the intended content count beyond structural lower bounds.

Benefits:

- later balance numbers attach to real content rather than placeholder names;
- exposes authoring and UI load before a numerical product lock;
- resolves which fixture distinctions are truly canonical.

Tradeoff:

- requires product/content direction that paper mechanics cannot derive;
- numerical fixtures remain provisional longer.

### `L-B — balance first with fixtures`

Next narrow exact paper values for Seed/sell prices, crop durations, Weather cadence and remaining cost/cadence fixtures while preserving all identity labels as fixtures.

Benefits:

- continues from the strongest A6/S18, Q4, WD/WS and ledger evidence;
- tests whether the structural package survives exact numerical choices before content naming expands.

Tradeoff:

- results remain paper baselines until canonical content is reconciled;
- greater risk of overfitting numbers to placeholder identities or content counts.

## 5. Owner-choice gate

| Choose | Next paper-design focus | Boundary that remains explicit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `L-I — identity/quantity first` | Canonicalize the minimum content identities and intended final count before exact balance lock | Current numeric values remain fixtures |
| `L-B — balance first with fixtures` | Narrow exact numeric paper baselines before canonical naming/content lock | Current names, identities and total content count remain fixtures |

Both are valid paper-design sequences. Existing evidence cannot select a product/content authoring order. `L-I` is the safer recommendation because it reduces the chance that exact numbers are optimized around content identities or counts that later change.
