## 2. `V0 — first-playable minimum lock`

Treat the selected inventory above as the complete first-playable Year 1 content set for the next balance lock. Additional crops, Catch, Persons, Projects and collection pages are deferred until the minimum loop is balanced and tested.

Benefits:

- every entry already has a distinct job;
- no filler is added before evidence shows a missing decision or repetition problem;
- exact prices, durations, Weather and cadence can be narrowed against a stable content count;
- smallest authoring, UI and comprehension surface.

Tradeoff:

- this is intentionally sparse and cannot claim final commercial variety;
- repetition/emotional breadth may be underestimated until later content expansion;
- fresh-player evidence still remains unavailable.

## 3. `V1 — broader content before balance`

Do not start exact numerical lock until Year 1 includes more than the structural minimum. New entries must first receive a real role and cannot be added only to make the list longer.

Benefits:

- balance can observe a more representative variety and content cadence;
- Person, crop, Catch and collection breadth may better express the life-sim fantasy;
- reduces the chance that prices/cadence are tuned around an unusually tiny catalog.

Tradeoff:

- no evidence currently identifies which extra slots are required;
- a new content-role audit must precede any count proposal;
- scope expands before the core numbers are stable;
- high risk of adding cosmetic variants that do not create new decisions.
