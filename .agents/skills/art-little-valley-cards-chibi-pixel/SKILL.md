# Art · Little Valley Cards Chibi Pixel

This is an independent, unapproved art study. Use the local [owner inspiration](assets/owner-chibi-pixel-reference.png) for broad visual cues and [our study board](assets/chibi-pixel-study-board.png) for the first Little Valley Cards comparison. Do not copy characters, objects, lettering, or composition from the inspiration image.

## Visual language

- Build tiny, stout, abstract silhouettes from intentional pixel clusters and a small number of shades per material. Keep hard pixel edges; do not apply a pixel filter to smooth illustrations.
- Make identity and gameplay state readable at roughly 160 × 160 logical pixels. A Hedgerow remains a rooted bush, a crop remains in soil, and a Farmhouse remains a place.
- Deliver whole 1:1 art at 512 × 512. Keep UI text, labels, frames and controls out of the raster. The tableau displays the whole square without cropping.
- Record source, prompt, local reference roles, processing, delivery image and `study` or `candidate` status beside each experiment. Do not assign a study to runtime without the owner's approval.

The approved MBE art direction has its own skill, `art-little-valley-cards-mbe`; do not merge the two rendering languages.
