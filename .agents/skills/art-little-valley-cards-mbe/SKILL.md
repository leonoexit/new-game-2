# Art · Little Valley Cards MBE

The owner approved the rounded-outline MBE direction for new Little Valley Cards art on 2026-09-26. This skill owns only that direction. Use its local [owner inspiration](assets/owner-mbe-reference.png) for broad visual cues and [our continuity board](assets/mbe-continuity-board.png) for the game's first exploration. Neither grants permission to copy characters, branding, or composition.

## Card-art contract

- Deliver a complete square `1:1` image at `512 × 512` for any card-art slot. Display the whole square with `object-fit: contain`; do not crop the subject, remove the background, or bake UI text and frames into art.
- Read at about `160 × 160` logical pixels. Identity and state must remain discernible without relying on tiny decoration.
- Keep card identity and gameplay meaning from the register and current contracts. For example, Hedgerow is a rooted bush, a mature crop stays in its soil context, and Farmhouse is a place.
- Record source, exact prompt, reference role, processing, delivered image and approval status beside each asset. A style approval does not automatically approve every generated asset.

## Approved MBE direction

Use the local references for rounded silhouettes, flat bright colors, a small number of colored contour strokes with deliberate gaps, sparse playful marks and short baseline accents. Add a tiny face only where it helps a living or crop subject. Preserve warm world character; do not turn every object or place into a mascot. Avoid gradients, clutter, generic emoji rendering, infographic labels, and copied characters or composition from the owner's inspiration image.

The whole square image may include its own quiet warm-white background. The game's name label, Type, State and interaction indicators are separate UI layers. On the tableau, the art fills its square box; the current UI study places the name at the lower edge. That label treatment is still under owner review, so do not bake it into art. Review artwork inside the actual tableau and full-card views before assigning it in the Card Register.
